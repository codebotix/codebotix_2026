ABOUT CODEBOTIX

CodeBotix is a technology education company helping children aged 8 to 17 learn AI, robotics, programming and emerging technologies through hands-on, project-based learning.

Our 1:1 live online classes give students the opportunity to experiment, build, solve problems and learn directly with experienced trainers. Students progress through structured programs designed to turn concepts into practical understanding.

We believe children should not just learn about technology. They should understand how it works, experiment with it and create with it.

Why We Started

CodeBotix began with a classroom in Louisiana.

In 2017, our founder experienced hands-on robotics education as part of the U.S. Department of State's Kennedy-Lugar YES Program. Instead of learning robotics from textbooks, students learned by building, testing, breaking and rebuilding.

That experience changed how he thought about education.

When he returned to India, he saw a different problem. Students were often able to assemble impressive projects by following tutorials, but many had little opportunity to understand what they were building or experiment beyond the instructions.

CodeBotix was created to change that.

Today, we use the same philosophy across our programs: learn the concept, experiment with it, build something, and figure out what happens when it doesn't work.
